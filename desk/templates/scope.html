How a report moves

new → triaging → (needs-info ⇄) → accepted → fixing → patched → disclosed → awarded → paid, or closed as duplicate, invalid, out of scope or withdrawn. Every move is in the log; every ticket says which internal ticket tracks its fix and which PR closed it.

Before, decide, later. The gate on a bounty ticket says whether its fix lands before mainnet, is a product decision still to be made, or can follow launch. The before list is the release view.
